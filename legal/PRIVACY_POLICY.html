LITTLE NO Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026 · Version 1.0.0

LITTLE NO is a single-player puzzle game. This build does not require an account and does not operate a game server. The Android package runs fully offline.

What we store

Progress, star ratings, and a mute preference are saved on your device only (the WebView’s local storage). We cannot read that data from our side because there is no backend.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or phone number
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, microphone, contacts, or files
  • Payment information
  • Chat or user-generated public content
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash or analytics logs

Third parties

This package ships with no ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no login. If a later version adds any of those, this policy will be updated before that version is published.

Children

The game is appropriate for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Clear the app storage to reset progress. Uninstalling the app removes local save data.
