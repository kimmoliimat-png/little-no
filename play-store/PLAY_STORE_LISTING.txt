FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
A dumpling named No walks forward forever. Toward cake. Toward peppermint fans. Toward a door marked OUT. You never steer it. You only rewrite the room.

Tap a board across a hole. Shut a lid. Freeze the jelly. Switch a fan. Undo if the universe disagrees.

LITTLE NO is a one-thumb portrait puzzle. Look at the problem. Change one thing. Watch the dumpling walk.

WHAT YOU DO
• You never grab the dumpling
• You tap fans, boards, lids, magnets, and belts
• You save it — or you watch the slapstick
• You try the next room because it said no

WHAT’S INSIDE
• 100 hand-built rooms
• Kitchen, Winter Lodge, Jungle Lodge, and Summer Lodge
• Candy-gloss worlds: cookie platforms, peppermint fans, macaron lids
• A squishmallow dumpling with a pink bow and a swirl lollipop
• Instant restart. Generous undo. No lives. No energy gates
• Stars for clean saves, not for paying

Made for short sessions. Designed so a fail looks as good as a win.

It does not listen. That is the entire game.
